# Dataset exploration

1,415 records across 34 batteries. 20 batteries end with a lower SOH than their first observed record. Individual traces may fluctuate; no measurements are clipped or removed.

SOH is capacity divided by first observed capacity for each battery in this CSV. RUL is the distance to the last recorded cycle, not a validated failure threshold. Correlation plots include derived targets and must not be interpreted as independent predictive evidence.
